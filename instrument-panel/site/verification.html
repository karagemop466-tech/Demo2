Collection errors

344 requests issued · throttle events (HTTP 429) observed: 0 · final spacing between request starts: 0.12 s.

Kalshi does not publish the size of the unauthenticated budget. It documents that public endpoints “charge your IP address’s budget first”, that a 429 carries no X-RateLimit headers when the response is publicly cacheable (which market data is), and that a 429 has no penalty or cooldown. The collector therefore spaces requests adaptively: it widens the gap on every 429 and narrows it slowly as calls succeed. The number above is the observed consequence, not a setting.

How a settled market is attributed to a topic

One documented approximation, stated plainly

Open markets are queried per series (/markets?series_ticker=…), so their series is exact. Settled markets are queried once per run with /markets?status=settled, which returns no series field; each settled market is attributed to the series whose ticker matches its event_ticker prefix (the event ticker is SERIES-EVENT by Kalshi's own naming). A settled market whose prefix matches no collected series is dropped rather than guessed, so the resolved count is a lower bound. Row-level source links still point at the exact market and the query that returned it.
