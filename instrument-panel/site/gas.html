Reading the bid/ask columns

A YES bid of $0.0000 with a YES ask of $1.0000 is not a missing value — it is the API returning the widest possible quote, which means nobody is currently bidding or offering that contract. The Liquidity column labels those rows empty book. Where a snapshot orderbook exists for the ticker it is shown on the Live & liquidity page, and an empty orderbook there confirms the same thing. A market can therefore carry large open interest and still be untradeable at any price today.
