Pick a market: the chart plots contract price against time with a window you choose, and the orderbook shows how many contracts sit at each price right now. Prices below refresh from your browser; the snapshot columns come from the last collector run.

Demo environment data — not real markets

Every price, volume and resolution below comes from Kalshi's demo exchange (external-api.demo.kalshi.co). Kalshi states that demo prices and behaviour “may not be reflective of those in real markets”. See demo environment docs. No real money is involved anywhere on this site.

Orderbook — contracts available at each price

Kalshi orderbooks contain bids only. A YES bid at $X equals a NO ask at $1−X, so the implied ask is computed as 1 − best NO bid. Levels are the API's own [price, contracts] pairs, sorted best-first.

Choose a market and load its orderbook. Snapshot orderbooks are shown further down.
