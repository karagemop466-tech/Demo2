Suggestions — review, then choose

Each suggestion is a decision waiting to be made, not a change that has been made. Read the trade-off, record a lean, and only then implement. The board stores your selection in this browser only (localStorage); it is not sent anywhere, and no suggestion is applied by this site.

Decision gate — apply to every card

  1. Is the missing information identified on the card actually missing, or is it already answered on the Questions page?
  2. Does the suggestion change what a reader would decide, or only how it looks?
  3. What is the cost of being wrong (wrong data shown as verified, credential exposure, misleading P&L)?
  4. Can it be verified from an official Kalshi source, and is that source linked?
  5. Is it reversible in one commit?

S1 · Run demo trade execution from a GitHub Actions job, not the browser

needs your decisionnothing implemented yet

Problem. Trading needs a private key. A private key cannot live in a public static site, so today's public site can neither read the portfolio nor place orders.

Recommendation. A
It is the smallest change that satisfies the brief without exposing a key: secrets stay in GitHub, the audit trail is the commit history, and the site remains static and safe. B is the right answer only if orders must be placed interactively within seconds.

Unlocks.

  • Portfolio balance and positions
  • Executed trades
  • Per-trade P&L with fees
  • Strategy categories grounded in real fills

S2 · Fix the definition of 'highest dollar amount'

needs your decisionnothing implemented yet

Problem. Kalshi exposes volume in contracts, not dollars. Three different rankings are defensible and they disagree: lifetime notional (volume × last price), 24-hour notional, and open interest in dollars.

Recommendation. A as the default, with all three visible and sortable
It is the only measure that is stable between refreshes and cannot be inflated by a single trade. Showing all three costs nothing and removes the ambiguity from the reader's path.

S4 · Verify and optionally add WebSocket streaming for the price chart

needs your decisionnothing implemented yet

How a chosen suggestion becomes a change

1Record the choice here (it is stored in your browser, under this site's origin).
2Download the JSON and attach it to an issue so the decision is on the record with the reasoning.
3Implement behind a small commit; the build validates data and links, so a broken choice fails the deploy.
4Anything still unconfirmed goes on Flagged for Review rather than being asserted.
