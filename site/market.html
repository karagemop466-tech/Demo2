Market Detail

Price-movement chart and liquidity. X-axis is time; Y-axis is the current contract price (cents). Liquidity shows contracts available at each price level from the live orderbook.

Price chart

Time granularity. Kalshi candlesticks support period_interval of 1, 60, or 1440 minutes. A true per-second candle is not available from the official candlestick endpoint; a seconds view would require the WebSocket trade channel (unverified — see Suggestion S5). Source: Get Market Candlesticks.

Liquidity — contracts available at each price

Liquidity. The orderbook returns yes_dollars and no_dollars price levels as [price, contracts] pairs, best bid last. In binary markets a yes bid at X equals a no ask at 100 − X. Cumulative depth is summed from the best price downward. Source: Get Market Orderbook.

Execute a trade

Trade execution uses the Kalshi V2 order endpoint and requires the authenticated backend with a demo API key (never committed). Source: Create Order V2.
