What is needed before P&L can be reasonably reported?

Measurement choices

  • Starting balance and time window
  • Realized vs. unrealized P&L
  • Fee and rebate inclusion
  • Deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and subaccount scope
  • How to reconcile live records to historical cutoffs

Strategy categories

  • Approved topic and market mapping
  • Trade intent / strategy label and version
  • Entry/exit rules, sizing, and risk limit
  • Outcome and settlement audit trail
  • Enough fills to evaluate results without cherry-picking

These are open requirements, not assumed values. See Questions, known answers, and missing information and the Flagged for Review list.

Secure implementation path: if portfolio tracking or orders are later approved, use an authenticated server-side integration with narrowly scoped credentials, audit logs, and explicit risk controls.
