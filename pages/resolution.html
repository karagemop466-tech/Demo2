4 · How resolution works on this exchange

  • A market has a status, a close_time / expected_expiration_time and, once settled, a result of yes or no plus settlement_value_dollars and settlement_ts — all documented on Get Markets.
  • Resolution is not always immediate after the close time: the demo API reports settlement_timer_seconds and the market may show as finalized later, so this tracker keeps polling rather than assuming an outcome at close.
  • The settlement source for each market is listed in the official series record (Get Series) — the demo environment uses its own sources, which is one more reason demo outcomes do not predict real ones.
