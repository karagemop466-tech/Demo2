How the events resolved, by topic

Counts of settled in-scope markets by the official result field. A market that never traded still has a result: it settles at $1.00 (yes) or $0.00 (no) regardless of whether anyone bought it, so it is counted here and flagged on Flagged for Review when its volume is zero — a YES on a market nobody traded is not evidence about anything.
