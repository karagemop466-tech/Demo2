Resolutions — tracked until the market resolves

Every in-scope market Kalshi has settled, with the official outcome (yes or no), the settlement value, and the settlement timestamp. Rows come straight from GET /markets?status=settled; the result field is Kalshi's own, not computed here.

Two official sources, labelled per row. strict series mapping is the collector's mapping through each series' official category/tags; settled-markets scan is a separate official settled-markets request whose topic comes from the market title. Both are Kalshi responses; rows are merged by ticker with the strict mapping taking precedence, and no result, price or timestamp is ever inferred. A market whose topic cannot be mapped is left out rather than guessed (see Flagged).
