Sharp-jump backtest

A transparent, simulated study of sharp price moves and entry-side calibration on the Kalshi demo environment. It reads only already-collected official demo data; every output row carries its source URL, and fields the API does not provide (the news or event cause of a jump) are left blank and flagged. This is historical demo analysis, not a tradable strategy and not investment advice.

Read the caveat first. The backtest enters at the final traded print of a resolved market. That price is not knowable at a decision time, so the numbers measure calibration-style edge on how the market resolved relative to its last print — they do not describe a strategy you could execute. Historical demo data only; demo prices may not reflect real markets.

Method

1 · Sharp-jump detector

Consecutive trade prints on the same ticker whose YES price moves by ≥ $0.10 in either direction are flagged as a sharp move. The detector runs over the trade history collected from GET /markets/trades. The Kalshi Trade API returns price and volume but no news/event field, so the cause of each jump is not inferred — every jump row is tagged cause unverified and links to the market's own page on Kalshi.

2 · Simulated strategy backtest

On resolved markets that actually traded (volume_fp > 0 and last_price_dollars > 0), buy 1 contract at the final print price and settle at $1.00 / $0.00 per the official result field. Four fixed entry sides are compared:

  • Always YES — buy the YES side of every traded resolved market.
  • Always NO — buy the NO side of every traded resolved market.
  • Favorite — buy whichever side was priced higher (closer to $1.00) at the final print.
  • Underdog — buy whichever side was priced lower (closer to $0.00) at the final print.

P&L is per contract: a winning contract pays $1.00, a losing contract pays $0.00, so per-contract P&L = (result matches side ? 1.00 : 0.00) − entry price. Fees are not modelled; the demo API does not expose a per-fill fee field used here.

Sources & tooling

The detector and backtest are implemented in session-8b9a46b4/tools/jump_backtest.py and run offline over data/dashboard_instrument.json. The generated data lives in session-8b9a46b4/data/backtest.json and session-8b9a46b4/data/jumps.json. Source endpoint: GET /markets?status=settled and GET /markets/trades. The same $0.10 jump threshold drives the live chart markers on the dashboard.
