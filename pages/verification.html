What is deliberately not claimed

Not claimedWhy not
That demo prices predict production pricesKalshi documents that demo prices may not reflect real markets.
A dollar-volume figure presented as exactNo such field exists in the Trade API; the dashboard labels its figure an estimate.
Ask-side order-book depth as a raw fieldThe API returns bids only; the ask side is derived as 1 − p and labelled.
A strategy derived from a handful of fillsThe analysis refuses to emit below 5 resolved fills.
Portfolio figures without credentials“No key” and “zero balance” are different facts, and are shown differently.
