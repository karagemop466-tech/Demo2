Executive Summary

Purpose. This site collects, organizes, and analyzes Kalshi demo markets in five topics only — gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, golf — tracking live events until resolution (yes/no), visualizing price movement and liquidity, and supporting demo-account portfolio tracking and trade execution.

What it does

  • Ranks open in-scope markets by notional (volume × last price) from Get Markets.
  • Charts contract price over time (seconds/minutes/hours) and shows orderbook depth via Get Market Orderbook.
  • Tracks settled markets and their result (yes/no).
  • With demo API keys, loads balance/positions/fills and places limit orders (signed per Authenticated Requests).
  • Categorizes fills and derives strategies only from observed data.

What it does not do

  • No real-money trading. Demo only (demo env).
  • No hard-coded market values — all numbers come live from the API or stay blank.
  • No strategy is recommended without fill data.

Key risks

  • Demo prices “may not be reflective of those in real markets” (source).
  • Topic mapping is keyword-based and heuristic — see Flagged for Review.
  • Trading requires your own demo API keys; keys never leave your browser.
