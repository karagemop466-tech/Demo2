Executive summary

What this system does, what it proves, and what it cannot yet do

A single dashboard over the Kalshi demo environment covering five topics (gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, golf), with live prices, liquidity, resolution tracking, a verified data snapshot, and an explicit register of everything that is still unverified.

What is verified and working

  • Scope from the official taxonomy. Markets are assigned to a topic using Kalshi's own series category and tags, published by Get Tags for Series Categories. No title-keyword guessing.
  • A live snapshot pipeline. Get Markets, Get Events, Get Series List and Get Market Orderbook are collected by scripts/fetch-data.mjs on a GitHub Actions schedule; every request is logged in data/collection-log.json. Snapshot built: 2026-11-20 11:45 UTC.
  • Real price history. The chart uses Kalshi candlesticks (Get Market Candlesticks) for the x-axis history and live REST snapshots for the newest points.
  • Liquidity at a price. The order book shows contracts resting at each bid price.
  • Resolution tracking. Settled markets are listed with their official result (yes / no) and settlement value.

What is deliberately not claimed

  • No fabricated numbers. Any field absent from an API response is rendered as UNVERIFIED, never guessed.
  • No real-money trading. Everything runs against the demo environment (demo env); Kalshi warns demo prices may not reflect real markets.
  • No account data without credentials. Portfolio and trade screens stay blank until a demo API key is supplied in the browser; see Portfolio &amp; trading.
  • No strategy without trades. Strategy suggestions stay empty until real fills exist.
