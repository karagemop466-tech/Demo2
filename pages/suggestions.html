Suggestions — review before anything changes

Eight improvement proposals, each with the problem, the options, the trade-off and the official source that motivates it. Nothing here is implemented on your behalf. Record a decision per item; decisions and notes stay in this browser so you can review the set as a whole before choosing a path.

Recommendations are advisory. Where a recommendation is already built (for example official-tag scoping) the card says so, so you can accept or reverse it with full knowledge. No decision is transmitted anywhere.

S1 · Decide what “highest dollar amount” means

Problem. The Trade API has no dollar-volume field. The dashboard currently ranks by volume_fp × last_price_dollars and labels it an estimate.

Options on the table.

  • Keep the estimate, clearly labelled (current)
  • Rank by volume_fp (contracts) only
  • Rank by open_interest_fp (contracts)
  • Rank by volume × time-weighted average price, collected over N runs

Recommendation. Keep the estimate, and add volume-only and open-interest-only sort keys — already done on the dashboard, so the reader can compare.

Effort Low · Impact High · Risk: A wrong definition silently changes every ranking.

Source: https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-markets

S2 · Scope by official tags, not keywords

Problem. Keyword matching on titles mislabels and misses markets.

Options on the table.

  • Official series tags/categories (implemented)
  • Manual allow-list of series tickers
  • Keyword matching (rejected)

Recommendation. Implemented. Gas is found by the “Gas” tag and golf by “Golf” because their categories are Economics and Sports; elections, culture and crypto use their category. Unmapped series are listed, never guessed.

Effort Low · Impact High · Risk: Model risk from mis-scoped markets.

Source: https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-series-list

No decision recorded

S3 · Raise data freshness from 10 minutes to live

Problem. GitHub Actions snapshots run every 10 minutes, so the 60-second chart window is usually empty and “live” is really “near-live”.

Options on the table.

  • Accept 10-minute snapshots (current)
  • Host a small relay that keeps a WebSocket open to the demo feed and appends ticks
  • Move to polling every 60 s (rate-limit budget permitting)

Recommendation. Approve a relay only if sub-minute responsiveness is actually required; otherwise rename the panel “near-live” so the label is honest.

Effort Medium · Impact High · Risk: A relay needs hosting, secrets and a cost owner.

Source: https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/quick_start_websockets

No decision recorded

S4 · Supply a demo API key pair

Problem. Portfolio, P&L and execution are blank because no demo credentials are configured. Nothing is fabricated to fill the gap.

Options on the table.

  • Add KALSHI_DEMO_API_KEY_ID and KALSHI_DEMO_PRIVATE_KEY as repository secrets
  • Leave read-only and public data only

Recommendation. Add a demo-only key pair as repository secrets.

Effort Low · Impact High · Risk: A leaked key; mitigate by using a demo-only key and keeping it in Actions secrets, never in the repo.

Source: https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/api_keys

No decision recorded

S5 · Set the evidence threshold for calling something a strategy

Problem. Any rule derived from a handful of demo fills is noise.

Options on the table.

  • 5 resolved fills, low confidence (current)
  • 20 resolved fills, medium confidence
  • 50 resolved fills, higher confidence
  • No strategies until a stated benchmark exists

Recommendation. Keep 5 as the floor but never label anything above “low” until 20 resolved fills per category.

Effort Low · Impact Medium · Risk: Overfitting to a demo market with mock liquidity.

Source: https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/demo_env

No decision recorded

S6 · Decide whether demo behaviour may inform production strategy

Problem. Kalshi documents that demo prices and behaviour may not reflect real markets.

Options on the table.

  • Treat strategies as demo-only exercises (current)
  • Re-validate each rule on production data before use
  • Ignore demo results entirely

Recommendation. Re-validate on production data before any real-money use.

Effort Low · Impact High · Risk: Transferring demo edges to real money.

Source: https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/demo_env

No decision recorded

S7 · Add resolution alerting

Problem. Resolution is recorded on the next 10-minute run, but nobody is notified.

Options on the table.

  • Email/Slack via Actions on newly settled in-scope markets
  • No alerts, dashboard only (current)

Recommendation. Add one digest per settled batch rather than per market.

Effort Low · Impact Medium · Risk: Alert fatigue if the topic list widens.
