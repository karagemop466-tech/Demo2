Simulated strategy desk

SIMULATED — not the demo account. Every fill below was placed by this repository's own code at the posted quote taken from the collected snapshot. No order was sent to Kalshi, nothing was executed on the demo account, and the order book was not consulted, so execution at the full simulated size is an assumption. Fees are a model, not an exchange confirmation. The real account is reported separately on Portfolio. Details and the exact assumptions are in the method table below.

Why so few (or no) fills

A rule that fires and produces no order is not the same as no opportunity. The ledger records how often that happened and why, so an empty book is visible rather than implied.

Method and assumptions

What the desk takes as given, and where each number comes from. Anything that cannot be confirmed is left blank and flagged above rather than estimated.

ItemBasis usedStatusSource
Entry / exit priceThe market's official yes_ask_dollars for a YES buy and no_ask_dollars for a NO buy, read straight from the collected snapshot. A side whose ask is not strictly inside (0, 1) is skipped — the desk never invents a price. Official fieldget-markets
SizingA fixed notional per position (see the tool), converted to whole contracts at the ask, so the number of contracts is derived arithmetic rather than a reported value. Derivedget-markets
Feesfee = ceil(0.07 × series fee_multiplier × contracts × p × (1 − p)) to six decimals, where the multiplier is the series' own fee_multiplier captured by the collector. The 0.07 constant is the general quadratic schedule and is not restated on the docs site, so it is labelled a model wherever it appears. model, not exchange-confirmed
fee_basis: “quadratic model, 0.07 constant — not exchange-confirmed” — the label written on every ledger row		 fee rounding, get-series
Marking open positionslast_price_dollars; a NO position is marked at 1 − p, which is the binary-market equivalence, and is labelled derived. Official field + derived sideget-markets
SettlementThe market's official settlement_value_dollars, falling back to the official result field. A non-binary or absent result leaves the position open and sets needs_review instead of being guessed. Official fieldget-markets
Order bookNot consulted. Fills assume the full simulated size is available at the posted quote, which is optimistic; a real order could be filled worse or not at all. assumption get-multiple-market-orderbooks
Demo-account performanceThis ledger is separate from the real account. No demo API key exists in this repository, so the account itself stays “not configured”. not the demo account api_keys
