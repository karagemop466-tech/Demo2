Trade analysis and strategy formation

Two layers: what the verified snapshot says about the in-scope markets, and what your demo fills say about your trading. Strategy rules are only produced once real fills exist — until then the correct output is “no strategy”.

These are hypothesis templates for tagging, not validated strategies. They are only reported on with observed fills, and they must be tested out of sample before use. Demo prices may not reflect real markets (demo env), so results do not transfer.
