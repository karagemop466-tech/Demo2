Rule used here. A segment is only written up as a strategy when it contains at least three fills in settled markets. Below that threshold the row is left empty on purpose — a pattern drawn from one or two trades is noise, and presenting it as a rule would be a guess.

4 · What this analysis does not cover

  • No comparison with a benchmark (no Kalshi index or market-average benchmark is published for the demo environment).
  • No slippage or fee-model projection beyond the fee the exchange itself reports per fill.
  • No attribution to “why” a market moved — the demo API does not publish a causal feed for price changes.
  • Demo fills happen against synthetic liquidity, so execution quality measured here does not transfer to the live exchange.
