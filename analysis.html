ANALYSIS THAT NEEDS ACCOUNT DATA

Trade review framework

Once authorized fills and settlements exist, classify each trade by market topic, YES/NO side, entry price, contract quantity, time to close, strategy tag, and settlement result. Evaluate net P&L after fees, win/loss frequency, average return, drawdown, exposure concentration, and performance by topic and strategy. Compare decisions to contemporaneous prices rather than hindsight.

No results are calculated here because no account data is available. Grouping suggestions are not validated trading strategies and should be tested out-of-sample with risk limits before any use.
