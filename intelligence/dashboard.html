How ranking works (honest version)

Live mode: for each watchlist series (verified top-volume series per topic plus series proven active during collection), the browser pulls GET /markets?series_ticker=…&status=open&limit=1000, groups markets into events, fetches official event titles via one GET /events?tickers=… CSV call, then ranks client-side by the derived estimate above. Snapshot mode: the same table renders from the verified 2026-11-20 capture; it is a subset (documented on each row's source link) and does not claim completeness. Both modes display blanks as blanks. Auto-refresh: 60 s (keeps request volume modest — see rate-limit discussion in Q&A).
Currency caveat: the demo environment explicitly states market prices and behaviour “may not be reflective of those in real markets” — official demo docs. Dollar figures here are demo dollars.
