Trade analysis & categorisation — public feed demo no keys required

The same categorisation engine that will run on personal fills is demonstrated here on the captured public trade feed (official GET /markets/trades rows). Categories come from verified fields only: topic via official series-category mapping; direction via taker_outcome_side (exposure) + taker_book_side; role taker always for public prints.

CategoryDimensionExecutionsContractsAvg YES priceNotes

Categorisation rules (documented): topic = official series category/tags of the market's series (unclassified when metadata was not fetched — never guessed); direction = taker exposure (yes/no from taker_outcome_side); price band = the executed yes_price_dollars (deep no <$0.10 · mid $0.10–0.50 · yes-leaning >$0.50 — thresholds are project conventions, shown explicitly). Source rows: data/snapshot.json → trades_sample (each row carries its endpoint).

Strategy templates by trade type

Templates are grounded in verified API mechanics (order types, settlement rules, fee fields). Each states what data it needs before being judged effective — judgement is withheld until credential-gated performance data exists (see Q1).

🧊

1 · Maker liquidity (post_only)

Type: maker · Mechanic: post_only orders rest and never cross; the quadratic fee model (fee_type=quadratic, fee_multiplier=1 on series) plus maker fill role from is_taker=false. Measure: maker fills' share of volume, spread captured vs adverse selection. Need: fills with is_taker=false.
spec

⚡

2 · Taker momentum (IOC)

Type: taker · Mechanic: immediate_or_cancel takes liquidity when the feed shows directional prints (e.g. clustered taker_outcome_side=yes prints like the captured DWTS sequence). Measure: P&L per taker fill vs price move after 60 s (1-minute candles). Need: fills + candlesticks.
spec

🏁

3 · Settlement capture

Type: event settlement · Mechanic: buy the near-certain outcome below $1 after determination — official settlement pays winners exactly $1/contract, zero settlement fee for simple yes/no. Measure: (1 − entry) × contracts captured vs opportunity cost of tied-up collateral. Need: settlements ledger.
spec

📐

4 · Range/strike basket

Type: multi-strike · Mechanic: range events list complementary strikes (observed WTI $77.00 band, BTC $54,200–54,499 bands); if asked-prices across the covering set sum <$1 the basket is theoretically cheap — verify against price_ranges before acting. Measure: basket cost vs $1 payout. Need: orderbooks for every strike of an event.
spec

Strategy effectiveness is intentionally unjudged: with zero credential-gated fills/settlements collected, claiming any edge would violate the no-inference rule. The templates and metrics are ready; the verdict arrives when Q1 is answered.
