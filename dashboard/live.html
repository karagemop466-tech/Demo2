Liquidity: resting orders by price

The orderbook lists only bids. A YES bid at price p is equivalent to a NO ask at 1 − p, so the “YES ask” column below is the NO-bid ladder reflected at 1 − p (per Kalshi’s orderbook docs). Contracts = number of resting contracts at that price.

Limits, stated plainly: (1) Candlesticks are 1-minute, 1-hour or 1-day only (docs), so “seconds” resolution comes from the Live tape, which records each poll.
