How to read this page

  • Resolved is the API result value. “YES” means the rule’s condition was met; “NO” means it was not.
  • Settlement timing (per docs): markets typically settle shortly after expiration, but timing varies by market type, data-source availability and review. Market settlement docs ↗
  • Sample size is small (6 real-series markets). No conclusions about win rates are drawn from it.
