Known restrictions

  • Only gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, and golf are in scope.
  • Authenticated WS requires signed headers; this browser-only page does not place credentials in the browser or connect to the authenticated WebSocket. Public REST polling is used where permitted. See WebSocket connection docs.
  • All money is demo/mock funds. No live or real-money order is placed by the Pulse tools.
