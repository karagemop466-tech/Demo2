Highest-dollar live events — five topics only

Live Kalshi demo markets for gas prices, elections, culture, crypto, golf only. Ranked by computed dollar notional (official contracts × official last price — the API has no dollar-turnover field), charted by contract price over time, with contracts available at each price, tracked until each market resolves yes or no. Simulated trades run automatically — no manual input.

Demo only. Mock funds. Demo prices “may not be reflective of those in real markets” (demo environment docs). Nothing here is investment advice.

Executive summary — important details

  • Scope: 174 in-scope series; 1,556 open markets collected; 1,601 resolved (317 ever traded). Snapshot … Source: demo_env.
  • Highest dollar: elections dominate (top ≈ $15.8k lifetime notional). Strict gas-price markets max ≈ $2.89 — thin demo liquidity. Culture has 0 open markets in the snapshot (2 series, 1 resolved). See Flagged P-F02.
  • Chart: X = time (60 s / 5 m / 15 m / 1 h / 6 h), Y = contract price $0–$1 fixed. Seconds are browser-polled observations (candlesticks exist only at 1 m / 1 h / 1 d — candlesticks).
  • Jumps: 39 moves ≥ $0.10 found in trade prints. External news causation is unverified (the API does not provide it) — each jump links to its Kalshi event, market, rules and settlement source instead of an invented news URL.
  • Simulated trading: 5 strategies backtested on 317 traded resolved markets + 12 live paper positions. Entry uses the final traded price (hindsight) — descriptive calibration, not a tradable strategy. Realised P&L stays blank until Kalshi settles.
  • Demo account: no API key in this repo, so balance/positions/fills are blank (not zero). Paper portfolio is shown separately and never mixed with account data.

How to use this dashboard

  1. Live chart — pick a market, choose a window, read liquidity beside it.
  2. Jumps — every ≥ $0.10 move with before/after prices and cause-context links.
  3. Simulated trading — strategy backtest + paper portfolio, categorised by topic/side/band.
  4. Resolutions — how each tracked market resolved (yes/no).
  5. Verification — every claim with its official source. Flagged — irregularities for review.
